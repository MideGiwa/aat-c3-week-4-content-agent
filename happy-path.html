Happy Path

AI Content Research & Publishing Agent — the request that sails through cleanly.

1

Content request submitted

Idea/topic, target audience, optional source URL and tone.

Gate: required fields present
2

Research & retrieval

Firecrawl scrapes the URL (or search finds 3–5 sources for a raw idea); text is chunked and embedded.

Gate: ≥1 usable source found
3

Source curation & planning

Relevant excerpts are selected; outline built with a keyword and a source mapped to every section.

4

Draft generation & self-evaluation

Article options are generated and scored against the rubric.

Gate: evaluation status = pass
5

Human review

Reviewer sees the draft, sources, and evaluation history — and approves it.

Gate: explicit approval recorded
6

Channel adaptation

Approved article is adapted into LinkedIn and X versions per the formatting rules.

7

Publishing queue → live

Assets land in the queue; the n8n runner claims and publishes them on schedule.

✓ Published to LinkedIn and X, fully traceable back to its sources
